About Momo Corner

Our Story, Our Flavour

At Momo Corner, we believe great food begins with great passion. What started as an unyielding love for authentic Himalayan street delicacies has grown into our commitment to serving piping-hot, flavor-packed momos that bring smiles with every single bite.

Every dumpling is handcrafted each morning using fresh farm vegetables, quality meats, fragrant herbs, and finely ground Himalayan spices. Whether you crave the gentle delicacy of bamboo-steamed dumplings, the exhilarating crunch of Kurkure, or the charcoal-kissed warmth of clay-oven tandoor, our kitchen prepares every order fresh with zero compromises.
